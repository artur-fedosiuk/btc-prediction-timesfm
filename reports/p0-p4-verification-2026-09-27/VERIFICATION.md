## D. Storage architecture

See `schema.md`. SQLite provides multi-record transactions, foreign keys, uniqueness, immutable triggers and schema versioning. A readable JSON export accompanies the database. Important derived files use atomic replacement. The original CSV is not migrated or rewritten. SQL integrity checks and semantic/hash checks reject corruption; they do not constitute cryptographic protection from a privileged attacker.

## E. Temporal contract

See `pipeline.md`. All internal comparisons use aware UTC. An observation is a closed hourly OHLCV bucket; its `close_time` is the exclusive bucket boundary, not an invented last-trade timestamp. `available_at` equals collector retrieval, conservatively proving when the collector knew the value. Both availability and closure must precede cutoff. Origin is the final valid input close. Generation finishes after cutoff and before the first target. Forecast steps 1/4/8/12/24 map exactly to origin plus those hours. Verification occurs after the matching target and retains each provider's exact close.

## F. TimesFM contract

Distribution `timesfm==3.0.1`; checkpoint `google/timesfm-3.0-pytorch`; revision `43046b85ec22d584a13f8098c2ed39c889e129c2`. Input: 512 hourly BTC/USD closes converted explicitly to float32. Horizon: 24 hourly steps. CPU batch size 1, seed 0, two threads, symmetric averaging. Full model configuration and inference options are persisted by the new adapter. Quantiles are nominal pointwise q10/q90 (80%), with no BTC calibration claim. Model failures do not silently invoke ARIMA.

`model-smoke.json` proves a real local checkpoint load and 24 valid outputs on a deterministic synthetic ramp. It does not prove forecast skill or a live market prediction. An additional preflight defect was found: macOS uses 16 KB pages here, but the existing checker assumed 4 KB and understated available memory fourfold. That calculation was corrected before loading; `preflight.json` records the corrected evidence.
